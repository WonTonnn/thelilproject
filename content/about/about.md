This is an educational resource written by students for students. All content are written and cited using peer-reviewed literature and resources from reputable scientific organizations. Concepts are simplified for accessibility while preserving scientific accuracy. When evidence is uncertain or actively debated, uncertainty is explicitly discussed.

**the dictionary project**
As much as we all want to read super cool articles about astrophysics, there's a massive, invisible gap between the researcher and the student. There's an implicit assumption that the reader understands terms ranging from first year high-school mitosis to graduate school level quantum physics. 

> I don't know about you, but I don't speak PhD

So here's the dictionary project: a glossary of terms and definitions that are linked to each of the articles!  

[PICTURE] These links; clickable! accessible! wonderful!

With the purpose of improving communicability and accessibility to difficult topics, I hope this'll help you digest tough topics that PI's seem to absorb like a Bounty towel (not sponsored).

As a new student, I'm pretty much learning right along-side you, so expect tons of simplification and way too many definitions; except I'll do all the heavy lifting for the both of us. Any definition suggestions can be sent here: [link to a google uhhh questionaire]
